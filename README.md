# 🎮 AI-Minecraft (Web Version MC Remake)

- A cyber miracle conjured up by "Moxixi who can't code" and "AI forced to work".

## 📜 Project Introduction

This is not just a webpage; it's a cyber epic depicting the mutual torment and love-hate relationship between humans (carbon-based beings) and AI (silicon-based beings) in the middle of the night.
This project perfectly replicates the rough feel of the early version of Minecraft in 2009, and incorporates "minimalist textured graphics" with distinct futuristic characteristics.

## ✨ Core Features (Unparalleled Edition)

* **Minimalist textual UI (eye protection)**: A wooden stick is represented by an 'I', a wooden pickaxe by a 'T', coal by a black 'C', and crude iron by a light gray 'Fe'. We learn the periodic table of chemical elements in Minecraft.
* **Hardcore container physics**: A realistic crate system. Once items are stuffed inside, even if the crate is smashed, it will obediently spit out the contents, never swallowing gold.
* **Anti-pinch and anti-theft door**: A tight AABB collision volume. When you stand inside the door frame and try to close the door, the system will gently push you out, ensuring you won't get stuck in the wall and suffocate.
* **Real sunlight damage**: Dark green rectangular zombies only appear at night. If exposed to sunlight after dawn, they will continue to lose health, and even drop "D" steaks (which can restore health and are the preferred choice of cyber cannibals) upon death.
* **"Little Universe" Boundary Protocol**: Following the mechanism of Xueyuntianming's Little Universe, when you reach the edge of the 64x64 map, you will be automatically teleported out from the other side, enabling seamless circular wandering.
* **Ancestral physics engine**: Jumping into water from a high place will never result in death.

## 🛠️ How to Run

1. Download this project to your local system.
2. Double-click `minecraft.html` to start your journey of creating the world.

*(Note: Currently, Three.js is still loaded from the CDN of unpkg.com. It is recommended to play when the network is stable.). )*
